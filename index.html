About the Trendline

The orange curve and shaded bands above are from Bitcoin's Gold Price: History, Model, and Falsifiable Predictions through 2035 (Biggs, 2026), which fits a saturating exponential model to the Bitcoin/Gold ratio. The model predicts Bitcoin will plateau near 40–45 oz of Gold by the mid-2030s. The bands widen before 2023 (volatile early era) and tighten after (reduced volatility regime). The chart below tracks whether the prediction is holding up.

Each month, the model's prediction error is standardized and added to a running total. If that total drifts outside a boundary, the hypothesis is rejected at α = 0.05. Boundaries are calibrated via Monte Carlo (2M simulations, 120 monthly looks) and account for the observed boom-bust autocorrelation (ρ = 0.90). See Section 10 of the paper for full methodology.
